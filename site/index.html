Your League games, explained.

Teeto is a free companion app for League of Legends on Windows. It sits next to your client and gives you useful info before, during and after your games. No ads, no account, nothing to log in to.

Download for Windows Free and open source · Windows 10 and 11
Champion select

Know who you're playing with

Your teammates' rank, mastery and recent form as soon as they show up, plus the build for your pick and one click to set your runes and summoner spells.

In game

One bar with what matters

Your K/D/A, CS per minute, gold lead, objective timers and core items in a single bar over the game. Switch parts off, fade it or move it anywhere.

Match history

Every game, explained

A full breakdown of every game with damage, gold, vision, objectives and a gold lead graph, plus badges for the moments that mattered, like a comeback or an MVP game.

LP tracking

See where your LP went

Every ranked game you play with Teeto open is saved with the LP you won or lost, on a graph you can scroll back through.

Champion stats

Tiers from real ranked games

Win, pick and ban rates per role from thousands of ranked games each patch, refreshed several times a day.

Patch notes

Patch notes for your champions

Teeto points out the changes to champions you actually play, so you don't have to dig through the full notes.

Stays on your PCTeeto reads what your League client already shows you. Your games are saved locally.
Doesn't touch the gameNo memory reading, no injection, no automation. It never asks for your password.
