Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Short version: Teeto has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. What it reads from your League client stays on your PC.

Who is responsible

The controller for this website and the Teeto app is the person named in the Impressum. You can reach us at eimmake@dev-dra.de.

The Teeto app

Teeto reads data from the League of Legends client and the in-game Live Client Data API that run on your own computer. This includes your Riot ID, rank, match history, champion select and the current game. Teeto saves your games and LP changes in a local database on your PC so it can show your history. None of this is sent to us. To remove it, uninstall Teeto and delete the gg.teeto.app folders in %APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA%.

To show augment tiers in ARAM Mayhem, Teeto takes screenshots of the game window and compares them on your PC. The last screenshot is kept locally for troubleshooting and is never uploaded.

Teeto loads public files from these services. Like any web request, they see your IP address and may log it under their own policies. No personal data from your League client is sent with these requests.

  • GitHub for app updates and downloads (privacy statement)
  • jsDelivr for Teeto's champion and build statistics, which are aggregated and contain no player data (privacy policy)
  • Riot Games Data Dragon and CommunityDragon for champion, item and augment images and data
  • arammayhem.com for ARAM Mayhem augment pick rates

Teeto's statistics are built from public match data obtained through the Riot Games API. We only publish totals per champion, item and augment, never anything that identifies a player.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub privacy statement. This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no third-party fonts or scripts.

If you email us, we use what you send only to answer you.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Since Teeto keeps your data on your own PC, you can delete it yourself at any time.

Changes

If Teeto starts handling data differently, we will update this page before the change ships.
